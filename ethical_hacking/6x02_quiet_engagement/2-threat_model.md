# Threat Model, Lumen Industrial Systems

## Framework Mix

This preliminary threat model applies STRIDE to assess security threats against Lumen's documented application and infrastructure components, PASTA to connect those threats to industrial customer operations and contractual risk, and MITRE ATT&CK where a specific adversary technique is relevant. The assessment is based exclusively on the engagement introduction and the asset classes documented in the Rules of Engagement: the Lumen-controlled cloud platform, public API, internal administration panel, in-house demonstration edge gateway, and demonstration MQTT broker. No independent DNS, HTTP, service enumeration, or exploitation evidence has been supplied. Consequently, the listed threats are hypotheses requiring reconnaissance and validation, not confirmed vulnerabilities. Severity ratings represent preliminary business-risk priorities and must be revised when technical evidence becomes available.

## Prioritisation Note

The three highest-priority threats are unauthorised MQTT publishing, cross-tenant compromise, and exploitation of the public API. MQTT message manipulation could undermine the integrity of industrial sensor readings and cause customers to make decisions based on inaccurate operational information. Cross-tenant compromise could expose or alter sensitive information across customer boundaries, directly damaging confidence in Lumen's service isolation. Public API exploitation could provide an entry point into the platform and potentially affect both confidentiality and availability. These threats warrant early, carefully controlled validation because Lumen's three major industrial customer renewals are due within eight weeks and are linked to its Series A financing. NIS2 Article 21 creates supply-chain security expectations that customers may translate into contractual assurance requirements. The threat rankings are provisional: no exploitation or technical weakness is claimed as confirmed, and any subsequent assessment must remain within the authorised Lumen-controlled scope. Customer-deployed gateways, sensors, and industrial networks remain excluded regardless of technical reachability.
