# Commercial Pitch, Nordstrøm Power Group

## Commercial value

I recommend that Vanguard Security pursue Nordstrøm Power Group on the proposed terms. This is a strategically valuable opportunity, not simply another cybersecurity assessment.

Our initial engagement represents **€156,000 in proposed fixed-fee revenue**, covering 130 consultant-days across three phases. A separately authorised technical validation phase could generate an additional €72,000. More importantly, successful delivery would position Vanguard as a trusted security advisor to a €3.8 billion Scandinavian energy group operating across five jurisdictions.

The potential €800,000 annual retainer is not committed revenue, and we should not price this engagement on the assumption that it will materialise. Nevertheless, a credible three-year roadmap creates a natural opportunity for follow-on advisory, remediation assurance, and governance support.

This engagement could also strengthen our position in the Scandinavian energy and critical infrastructure market, where demonstrated operational understanding matters more than generic penetration-testing credentials.

## Risk profile and mitigation

The opportunity carries meaningful delivery and commercial risks. Our design addresses them before contract signature.

First, scope creep is controlled through a defined Statement of Work, explicit exclusions, and written change orders. Production OT exploitation and unrestricted subsidiary testing are excluded.

Second, inherited German acquisition audits create potential independence concerns. We will verify previous findings rather than endorse another firm's conclusions without evidence.

Third, the board deadline is non-negotiable. Weekly milestones, protected reporting capacity, and a Day 85 delivery target provide escalation time before Day 90.

Fourth, OT safety takes precedence over revenue. Initial assessments remain non-intrusive, with operational approval and immediate stop-work authority.

Fifth, subsidiary resistance is addressed through Elin Solberg's group-level sponsorship, named local contacts, and formal escalation.

Finally, five jurisdictions create legal complexity. Our regulatory specialist and external NIS2 legal advisor will validate applicable requirements, permissions, and data-handling boundaries.

These controls do not eliminate risk; they make it visible, accountable, and commercially manageable.

## Why this design, and confidence in execution

The obvious alternative is an expansive, group-wide penetration test. That would increase operational exposure, encourage uncontrolled scope growth, and threaten the board deadline. A documentation-only review would be safer but insufficiently credible.

Our phased, risk-based approach offers a stronger balance.

We prioritise German integration exposure, critical IT/OT boundaries, and Danish trading applications. PTES provides methodological discipline, while OWASP, MITRE ATT&CK, and NIST SP 800-53 support domain-specific assessment and governance mapping.

Five core consultants and two targeted external specialists provide the required expertise without committing a permanent oversized team. Fixed pricing protects client budget certainty, while contractual change control protects Vanguard's delivery economics.

A key lesson from past consulting engagements is central to our approach: ambiguity must be resolved before it leads to unpaid work or a damaged client relationship.

We have a commercially attractive opportunity, a defensible scope, and a realistic execution plan.

**I ask this committee to authorise submission of the proposal on these terms, subject to final legal and commercial approval.**
