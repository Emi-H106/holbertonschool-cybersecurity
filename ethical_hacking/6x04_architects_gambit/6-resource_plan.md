# Resource Plan, Nordstrøm Power Group

## Resource Planning Principles

Vanguard Security proposes a multidisciplinary engagement team designed to deliver Nordstrøm Power Group's initial cybersecurity assessment and three-year security roadmap within the ninety-day board deadline.

The staffing model reflects five distinct energy-sector subsidiaries, cross-border regulatory obligations, operational technology safety requirements, the incomplete integration of the German solar acquisition, and the need for clear communication with Scandinavian and German stakeholders.

The initial engagement comprises **130 consultant-days across twelve weeks**, distributed consistently with the approved scope architecture:

| Engagement phase | Schedule | Consultant-days |
|---|---|---:|
| Phase 1 — Strategic Baseline and German Integration Assurance | Weeks 1–3 | 36 |
| Phase 2 — Critical Operations and Trading Exposure Assessment | Weeks 4–8 | 64 |
| Phase 3 — Board Assurance and Three-Year Security Roadmap | Weeks 9–12 | 30 |
| **Total initial engagement** | **12 weeks** | **130** |

The proposed staffing model uses five core Vanguard consultants and two limited-duration external specialists. All external specialist days are included within the 130-day baseline, rather than added as unbudgeted work.

The initial cost baseline remains **€156,000**, using the illustrative blended rate of €1,200 per consultant-day established in the scope architecture. Actual external subcontractor fees, legal rates, travel expenses, and any specialist premiums require commercial confirmation before contract signature.

The optional fourth phase is excluded from the initial staffing and financial commitments.

**Internal consultant count: 5.**

The team deliberately prioritises experienced practitioners because the engagement includes safety-critical industrial environments, regulated energy operations, and executive-level assurance decisions.

Mid-level technical delivery is concentrated in application and API assessment, while senior consultants retain responsibility for scope decisions, OT safety boundaries, regulatory interpretation coordination, and final report quality.

Junior support may assist with document preparation under supervision if available, but no additional junior consultant-days are assumed in the approved baseline.

### Language and Stakeholder Requirements

**German-language capability — Required.** The IT and Identity Security Consultant must be able to conduct technical interviews and interpret relevant documentation in German. This requirement is particularly important for the recently acquired German solar subsidiary, where inherited infrastructure, supplier arrangements, and historical audit evidence may not be documented in English.

**Scandinavian-language capability — Preferred.** The Lead Engagement Architect should preferably communicate in a relevant Scandinavian language, particularly Norwegian, to support engagement with Nordstrøm's holding-level stakeholders. English may serve as the common working language only after stakeholder confirmation. Local-language support will be arranged where operational interviews or safety documentation require it.

**OT communication capability — Required.** The OT specialist must be able to communicate technical safety constraints clearly with local engineering and operations personnel. Any language gap affecting safety-critical interpretation must be resolved before assessment activities begin.

**Regulatory capability — Required.** The Regulatory and Risk Consultant must distinguish national NIS2 implementation requirements from Norway's applicable domestic framework and coordinate formal legal interpretation with the external advisor.

These language requirements are delivery controls, not optional conveniences. Misinterpreted interviews, safety instructions, or inherited audit evidence could invalidate assessment conclusions and generate additional work.

### Phase 1 — Weeks 1–3: 36 Consultant-Days

**Primary objective:** Establish the strategic baseline, confirm authorisations, assess group identity exposure, and examine German acquisition integration.

The Lead Architect coordinates stakeholder interviews, scope confirmation, Rules of Engagement, and risk governance.

The German-speaking IT and Identity Consultant leads inherited identity, VPN, privileged access, and integration trust reviews.

The Regulatory and Risk Consultant and external legal advisor examine the jurisdictional assessment assumptions and the provenance of inherited assurance evidence.

The OT specialist provides an early safety and architecture review to ensure Phase 2 assessment methods are appropriate for critical industrial operations.

**Phase 1 total: 36 consultant-days.**

### Phase 2 — Weeks 4–8: 64 Consultant-Days

**Primary objective:** Assess critical operational exposure across the subsidiaries and application/API security risks in Danish energy trading.

The OT / ICS Consultant leads non-intrusive reviews of Norwegian offshore gas, Swedish hydroelectric and wind, Danish offshore wind, Dutch gas and hydrogen infrastructure, and German solar-management architecture.

The external OT engineer supports selected equipment-specific safety and architecture reviews where operational expertise is essential.

The Application and API Consultant assesses Danish trading authentication, authorisation, API exposure, and relevant business-logic controls under approved non-disruptive conditions.

The IT and Identity Consultant investigates remote-access exposure, third-party connectivity, and cross-subsidiary identity dependencies.

The Lead Architect coordinates technical evidence, risk severity, operational boundaries, and progress against the board deadline.

**Phase 2 total: 64 consultant-days.**

### Phase 3 — Weeks 9–12: 30 Consultant-Days

**Primary objective:** Convert technical evidence into a defensible board-level assessment and three-year cybersecurity roadmap.

The Lead Architect consolidates the risk register, leads executive workshops, defines prioritised investment recommendations, and owns the final report.

The Regulatory and Risk Consultant completes control-gap analysis, remediation sequencing, risk ownership, and the regulatory evidence matrix.

The external legal advisor validates material jurisdiction-specific regulatory statements.

The external OT engineer reviews selected operational safety assumptions and the feasibility of recommended OT remediation measures.

The Lead Architect retains responsibility for final quality assurance, supported by the documented technical findings and specialist reviews completed in earlier phases.

The board-ready report is targeted for **Day 85**, leaving time for final stakeholder review before the ninety-day deadline.

**Phase 3 total: 30 consultant-days.**

## External Specialists (In / Out, with Rationale)

### External Specialist 1 — NIS2 Legal Advisor

**Decision: IN — Include as a limited-duration external specialist.**

**Seniority:** Senior legal advisor with expertise in European cybersecurity regulation, national NIS2 implementation, energy-sector obligations, and cross-border compliance.

**Allocation:** 6 consultant-days, comprising 4 days in Phase 1 and 2 days in Phase 3.

The legal advisor is required because Nordstrøm's regulatory exposure cannot be assessed accurately through a generic EU-wide NIS2 checklist. The engagement must distinguish national obligations in Sweden, Denmark, the Netherlands, and Germany from Norway's domestic regulatory framework. The advisor will validate jurisdiction-specific assumptions, identify matters requiring local legal confirmation, and review material legal statements before the board report is issued. Including this expertise from the beginning reduces the likelihood of unsupported compliance claims, late-stage report revisions, or additional legal work being requested outside the agreed scope.

The legal advisor does not replace the internal Regulatory and Risk Consultant and will not provide a blanket certification of compliance.

### External Specialist 2 — OT Engineer Subcontractor

**Decision: IN — Include for targeted specialist review, not continuous deployment.**

**Seniority:** Senior industrial control or energy-sector engineer with relevant operational safety and equipment architecture experience.

**Allocation:** 4 consultant-days, comprising 2 days in Phase 2 and 2 days in Phase 3.

Nordstrøm operates substantially different industrial environments, including offshore gas, hydroelectric generation, offshore wind, gas and hydrogen infrastructure, and solar facilities. A general OT security consultant may not possess equipment-specific expertise for every process. The external engineer will support safety-sensitive architecture interpretation, validate assumptions about operational dependencies, and review the feasibility of proposed remediation measures. This short, pre-budgeted allocation is preferable to assuming that specialist engineering knowledge can be obtained later through an unplanned change order.

The subcontractor will not independently perform intrusive production OT testing. All activities remain subject to Nordstrøm's operational approval, Vanguard's Rules of Engagement, and the supervision of the Lead Architect and OT / ICS Consultant.

**External specialist count: 2.**

**Total external specialist effort: 10 consultant-days, included within the 130-day initial engagement.**

## Resource Risks and Mitigations

| Resource risk | Potential consequence | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| German-speaking security consultant unavailable | Delayed German interviews and unreliable interpretation of inherited documentation | Confirm German-language capability before mobilisation and reserve qualified replacement capacity |
| OT specialist unavailable during Phase 2 | Incomplete safety assessment or delayed subsidiary reviews | Reserve senior OT capacity before contract signature and coordinate local engineering access |
| Country-specific legal expertise unavailable | Incorrect regulatory assumptions and late reporting revisions | Pre-book the external NIS2 legal advisor for Phases 1 and 3 |
| Industrial equipment expertise insufficient | Unsafe assumptions or impractical OT recommendations | Allocate targeted external OT engineering review within the baseline |
| Subsidiary stakeholders unavailable | Delayed evidence collection and missed assessment windows | Confirm named contacts and interview schedules during Phase 1 |
| German acquisition documentation incomplete | Unverified findings or expanded investigation needs | Maintain an evidence-confidence register and escalate missing records without silently expanding scope |
| Board deadline compression | Reduced report quality and insufficient executive review | Complete technical assessment by Week 8 and target board-ready delivery by Day 85 |
| Unplanned scope expansion | Consultant-day overrun and commercial disputes | Apply formal change control and separate additional work from the approved baseline |

Resource allocation will be reviewed weekly against actual effort, deliverable progress, access constraints, and remaining consultant-days.

Material changes in scope, specialist availability, or assessment depth will require documented approval before additional work begins.

## Optional Phase 4 — Targeted Technical Validation

The optional fourth phase remains a separately authorised engagement and is not included in the initial 130 consultant-days.

Consistent with the scope architecture, Vanguard may provisionally plan **60 additional consultant-days over approximately six to ten weeks**, subject to approved technical work packages.

Potential staffing includes:

- A senior penetration-testing lead.
- An IT / identity penetration tester for German integration validation.
- An application security tester for Danish trading systems.
- An OT / ICS specialist for controlled laboratory or otherwise authorised technical validation.
- Specialist engineering support where safety or equipment-specific expertise is required.

The final Phase 4 team size, allocation, testing permissions, and commercial terms must be determined through a separate Statement of Work and Rules of Engagement.

The indicative Phase 4 amount of €72,000 is a planning assumption, not an approved extension or a commitment to comprehensive testing across all five subsidiaries.

## Final Staffing Recommendation

Vanguard should mobilise a five-person internal consulting team supported by two pre-planned external specialists: a senior NIS2 legal advisor and an OT engineer subcontractor.

The model provides:

- Senior architectural oversight and executive accountability.
- German-language expertise for the acquired solar subsidiary.
- Preferred Scandinavian-language capability for holding-level engagement.
- Dedicated OT / ICS expertise for safety-critical energy operations.
- OWASP-aligned application and API assessment for Danish trading.
- Regulatory and control-assessment expertise across multiple jurisdictions.
- Targeted external legal and engineering review without uncontrolled staffing growth.

The complete initial plan requires **130 consultant-days across twelve weeks**, with 120 internal consultant-days and 10 external specialist-days.

The proposed resource allocation supports the agreed three-phase engagement, preserves the ninety-day board deadline, and anticipates language, regulatory, and industrial safety needs before they become delivery risks.

This approach applies Henrik Lindqvist's central lesson: the engagement must be staffed and bounded according to the client's actual requirements before contractual commitments are made, rather than relying on later change orders to compensate for predictable omissions.
