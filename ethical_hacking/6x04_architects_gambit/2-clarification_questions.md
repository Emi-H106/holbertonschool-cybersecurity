## Scope

1. Which subsidiaries, business-critical assets, and IT/OT environments must be independently assessed, and which can initially be evaluated through existing evidence? How should we prioritise offshore gas, renewable generation, trading, and hydrogen infrastructure?
   
   *Informs: Risk-based scope architecture, subsidiary selection, phase sequencing, and exclusions for the engagement design.*

2. Given the German solar subsidiary's recent acquisition and incomplete integration, what independent security validation has been completed, and which previous third-party audit findings remain reliable, accessible, and contractually reusable?
   
   *Informs: Whether to independently validate inherited findings, rely on existing evidence, sequence German integration testing separately, or defer unsupported conclusions; also determines conflict-of-interest safeguards.*

## Timeline

3. What specific decisions must the board make within ninety days, and what is the latest date for validated findings to reach Elin before the strategic plan is finalised?
   
   *Informs: Delivery milestones, assessment depth, reporting deadlines, and the division between the initial board-focused phase and later technical work.*

4. Which subsidiaries can guarantee access to asset inventories, technical contacts, and testing windows during the first thirty days, and what happens if approvals or operational windows are delayed?
   
   *Informs: Mobilisation schedule, dependency management, critical-path planning, and contractual treatment of client-caused delays.*

## Budget

5. Is the initial assessment funded separately from the potential EUR 800,000 annual retainer, and what budget ceiling or approval thresholds apply to discovery, specialist OT testing, and additional subsidiaries?
   
   *Informs: Fixed-price versus phased commercial structure, staffing allocation, optional work packages, and change-order thresholds.*

6. Would Nordstrøm prefer a minimum viable board assessment with separately priced expansion options, or a broader baseline with explicitly reduced testing depth?
   
   *Informs: Commercial trade-offs, depth-versus-coverage decisions, phase pricing, and defensible exclusions.*

## Constraints

7. What production OT activities, active exploitation techniques, service interruptions, and third-party systems are prohibited, and who has authority to approve, pause, or terminate testing in each subsidiary?
   
   *Informs: Rules of Engagement, safe testing methods, stop conditions, escalation procedures, and OT specialist requirements.*

8. Who can provide written authorisation for each subsidiary, and which national regulatory, data-handling, or contractual restrictions affect cross-border testing and evidence transfers?
   
   *Informs: Authorisation boundaries, jurisdiction-specific NIS2 mapping, evidence-handling procedures, and contractual responsibilities.*

## Board expectations

9. Does the board expect a compliance-oriented NIS2 baseline, a prioritised operational risk assessment, a three-year investment roadmap, or a combination of these with distinct acceptance criteria?
   
   *Informs: Report architecture, executive summary, regulatory mapping, remediation roadmap, and measurable deliverable acceptance criteria.*

10. Which business consequences should drive executive risk prioritisation: energy production disruption, worker safety, trading interruption, regulatory exposure, or integration risk, and who will validate those impact assumptions?
    
    *Informs: Environmental severity scoring, stakeholder interviews, risk prioritisation, and business-impact reporting.*

## Technical preferences

11. What existing architecture diagrams, asset inventories, vulnerability assessments, monitoring evidence, and previous audit reports can Vanguard access, and what confidence or freshness limitations should we apply?
    
    *Informs: Evidence-reliance criteria, discovery workload, independent verification requirements, and traceability of findings.*

12. Would Nordstrøm authorise a PTES-led assessment combining targeted OWASP testing for relevant applications, MITRE ATT&CK for adversary pathways, and ATT&CK for ICS for OT threat scenarios, subject to production-safety restrictions?
    
    *Informs: Methodology selection, technical testing depth, tool restrictions, specialist staffing, and the boundary between simulated and prohibited activities.*
