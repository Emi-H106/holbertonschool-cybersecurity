> **Section 4.2: Prohibited Activities**
>
> Users shall not use BioHealth Inc. systems, networks, servers, workstations, cloud resources, electricity, or any other company-owned resource to mine cryptocurrency or participate in any cryptocurrency-mining activity.
>
> Users shall not install, download, execute, or distribute software, scripts, browser extensions, or other executable code without prior written authorization from the IT or Information Security Department.
>
> Users shall not disable, bypass, modify, or interfere with security controls, monitoring systems, antivirus software, endpoint protection, access restrictions, or system configurations implemented by BioHealth Inc.
>
> Users shall not share passwords, authentication tokens, access cards, or other credentials, nor use another person's account to access BioHealth Inc. systems or data.
>
> Violations of this policy may result in the immediate suspension of system access and disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment. BioHealth Inc. may also report suspected illegal activity to the appropriate authorities and pursue civil or criminal legal action where permitted by applicable law.
